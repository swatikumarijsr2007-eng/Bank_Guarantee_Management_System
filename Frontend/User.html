Submission Guidelines for Outgoing Bank Guarantees

1

Fill BG Particulars

Select your operating department and branch location. Input the specific Work Order number associated with the project and define the Type of Work. Make sure the Job Value and requested BG amounts align with approved budget thresholds.

2

Observe Executive Approval thresholds

Our audit and validation system requires specific documents depending on the guarantee limit. For all outgoing guarantees up to ₹15,00,000 (15 Lakhs), an Executive Managing Director (MD) approval letter must be attached. For anything exceeding ₹15 Lakhs, a comprehensive Board Resolution document is strictly mandatory.

3

Draft Vetting & Clause Compliance

Upload a finalized legal draft of the BG in MS Word format (.doc/.docx) vetted by legal council. Ensure the corresponding Beneficiary Advertisement/BG requirements clause is attached so checking officers can cross-validate terminology.

4

Processing Timeline

Upon clicking submit, requests will immediately appear in your Tracking Log as 'Pending'. System reviews are typically completed within 3 to 5 business days, after which they will move into the 'Approved Queue' or be flagged with Remarks for adjustment.
